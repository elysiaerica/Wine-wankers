Usage rules

Never use all seven spectrum colours at equal weight. The spectrum appears as gradients, sequential bars, or 2–3 colour pairings. Yellow is the primary CTA accent. Red and violet are endpoints that anchor the visual range.

What this avoids

No sage green, terracotta, or millennial beige. No hot pink as dominant colour. No gradients that feel like tech startups. The palette should feel like a wine bar at golden hour, not a wellness brand.
