04 Typography

Warm italic, sharp functional.

Four typefaces, four distinct roles. Syne owns the hero. Instrument Serif brings editorial warmth to section heads. Newsreader handles body. JetBrains Mono labels everything functional. Crushky is for social graphics only.

Syne ExtraBold 800 — Hero & H1

Hero title, key statement lines, page H1s. Lowercase. Tracking -0.02em. Line-height 0.9. NOT for section headings.

Instrument Serif Italic — Section heads

Page section h2/h3s. Italic. Warm, editorial, human. Chapter titles throughout site.

Newsreader — Body

Paragraphs, tasting notes, event copy. Italic for quotes and support lines.

JetBrains Mono — UI / Labels

Uppercase labels, nav, CTAs, captions. Monospace precision against warm serif.

Crushky Brush — Socials only

Instagram event posts, physical posters, social graphics. Never in web UI, body copy, or email.
