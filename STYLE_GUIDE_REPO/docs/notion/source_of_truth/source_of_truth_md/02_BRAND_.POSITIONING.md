Core statement
The anti-pretentious pour club.
Community-first, anti-gatekeeping, welcoming to people who like wine but hate wine culture performance.

Hero line (locked)
good wine. weird people.
"good wine." is plain cream. Only "weird" carries the rainbow stack effect. Both lowercase.

Support line (locked)
"sick of wine snobs, spit buckets and sommeliers?"
Italic. Lowercase. One sentence that disarms wine-culture gatekeepers before they arrive.

Wine Wankers occupies the unclaimed centre: LGBTQ+ community × Natural wine culture × Local IRL events. No competitor holds this intersection.

Primary CTA (locked)
JOIN THE GANG — all caps, JetBrains Mono, glow button (rainbow activates on hover only, calm at rest)
