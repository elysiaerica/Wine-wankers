Typography, defined
Hero display — Syne ExtraBold 800
Used for hero title and key statement lines. Lowercase. Tight tracking -0.02em. Line-height 0.9.
Section headings — Instrument Serif
Used for section h2/h3 headings throughout page body. Often italic. Gives warm editorial quality to section titles.

Body copy — Newsreader
Serif, editorial, warm. Paragraphs, descriptions, support copy. Italic variant for quotes, support lines, and emphasis.

UI / labels — JetBrains Mono
All uppercase labels, badges, metadata, nav links, buttons, captions. Monospace, technical feel.

Social Content & Logo Fallback - Crushky — Instagram/physical print graphics & Logo fallback only. Always uppercase and never used for body text, headings, or UI elements.
