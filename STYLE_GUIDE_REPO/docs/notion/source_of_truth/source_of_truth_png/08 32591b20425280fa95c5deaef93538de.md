Tone of Voice
Anti-snob, not anti-expert
Disarms pretension. Does not dismiss knowledge — rejects gatekeeping. Warm, curious, slightly irreverent, never smug.

Community-first language
Lead with people, not product. "The room", "The Regulars", "weird people" — these are the story. Wine is the vehicle, belonging is the product.

Manifesto close
NO SNOBS · NO SPIT BUCKETS · NO SOMMELIER COURSE REQUIRED · JUST GOOD WINE & WEIRD PEOPLE · LOVE, WINE WANKERS xx

Copy nuggets
good wine for people who actually drink it · come for the wine, stay for the people · no prior knowledge required · 18 seats, always sells out · bring someone weird · wine belongs in a glass, not on a pedestal

Do say
good wine for people who actually drink it · come for the wine, stay for the people · no prior knowledge required · 18 seats, always sells out · bring someone weird · self-deprecating humour · short sentences · lead with people and energy not product · irreverence that signals confidence · "come drink good wine with us"

Don't say
curated selection · artisanal experience · premium wine community · exclusive access · sophisticated palate · terroir-forward · natural winemaking philosophy · immersive tasting journey · "we're passionate about..." · pét-nat / orange wine jargon in headlines · anything implying wine knowledge required · corporate warmth · wellness framing
